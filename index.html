crack-js

pure-javascript hashcat-format engine — verify · identify · generate · benchmark hundreds of hash modes with no server and no dependency beyond crypto-js. everything below runs in your browser.

“fast” = single-shot hash: unsalted, computed in one pass (md5, sha1, ntlm…) — millions/sec. “slow” = salted & iterated by design (bcrypt, pbkdf2, scrypt, argon2) — far fewer.
